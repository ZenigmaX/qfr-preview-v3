Questions, answered in plain language

For property owners and property managers. If your question is not listed, call 416-900-7998 or start a quote.

Quotes, scheduling, and the work

How do I request a quote

Use the quote request on this site. It asks for the service, what you are seeing, the property type, photos, the property address and city, weekday timing, and your contact details. You can also call 416-900-7998. We follow up about a weekday visit. We do not quote a price from the form or a chat alone.

Which areas do you serve

We work on properties in Mississauga, Oakville, Toronto, Brampton, Vaughan, and Hamilton. If the property is just outside those cities, still send the request and we will say whether we can take the job.

What are your hours

Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday. The phone line and site visits follow this schedule.

Do you work on residential and commercial properties

Yes. Describe the property and the work in the quote request, including any access notes for a multi-unit or commercial site.

What warranty, insurance, and WSIB coverage apply

5-year warranty on all work, $5 million liability insurance, WSIB coverage.

Do you install Christmas lights

Yes, as a seasonal service. We can install the customer's lights or lights supplied by QFR, and we also offer takedown and storage. Request a quote while the season is open.

Can I use the voice or text assistant to book

When Retell AI is connected, the voice and text assistants can take your name, phone, property address, service type, and preferred timing. If you have photos, they will point you to the quote form. They will not quote prices. If you ask, they will say they are an AI assistant, not a person.

Questions from property managers

How should a property manager request work

Use the same quote request. Put the property address and city, name the units or buildings if more than one is involved, and attach photos for each location when you can. Include your name and a daytime phone number, and note if access or keys need to be arranged. We schedule weekday visits only. For ongoing property maintenance beyond a single repair, use the separate property maintenance website once it is live. Service-agreement options are described on this site without prices. Use Request pricing to ask about them.

Can one request cover more than one unit or building

Yes. List each unit or building in the project details and attach photos labelled by location if possible. If the work should be billed to a management company, include that in the details so we can address the follow-up correctly.

Do you provide invoices or photos after the work for a property file

Yes, we can send an invoice and photos of the completed work for a property file when you ask for that on the request or after the visit. That is the Document stage of the Line Integrity Method.

Is there a different process for insurance-related fence damage

Use the quote request and mention that the work may be tied to an insurance claim. Include the property address and photos. We will discuss next steps after we review the damage. We do not quote prices on this page.
