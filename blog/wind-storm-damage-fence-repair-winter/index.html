What often fails in winter

Wind, snow load, and freeze-thaw movement can lean posts, split rails, lift panels, or twist a gate so it no longer closes. After a storm, walk the line, check posts at grade, and photograph any section that has moved: a wide shot of the run and a close shot of the failed post or panel.

How to request a weekday repair

Use the quote request, or start from the damage triage. Include the property address and city. Urgent winter repairs follow the same Monday-to-Friday schedule, not Saturday or Sunday.
